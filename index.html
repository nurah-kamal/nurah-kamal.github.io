      How I got here

      My love for data started with maths, and probably with sudoku, which I loved as a child. That’s where the pattern-spotting began. The more maths I did, the more I realised that what I enjoyed most was spotting patterns and analysing them. That turned into a love for statistics, and statistics led me to data.

      My job has given me the chance to work with real operational data: working out why something is happening, finding the pattern, and then building something that makes the process easier. The end result isn’t really the dashboard or the script. It’s that someone can work differently.

      How I work

      • Check the source before trusting the number.A number that looks right isn’t necessarily right. I go back to where the data came from, check what’s actually being measured and look for inconsistencies.
      • Understand the problem before automating it.Otherwise you just get the wrong answer faster.
      • When I’m stuck, I stop forcing it.I break the problem down, isolate the part that doesn’t make sense and test one thing at a time.
      • Data about people deserves care.Most of what I work with is about real people, so privacy (POPIA) is part of the design from the start.
      • Presentation matters.A report that’s accurate but hard to read hasn’t quite done its job.
