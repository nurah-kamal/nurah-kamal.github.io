Off the map

My projects are on the map. This page is everything else: what I believe, what I’m figuring out, and the little things. It changes as I do.

Last updated 25 October 2026

  1. A snapshot of now

    Currently in my era of building things, learning things, and figuring out what’s next.

    • Exploring data science and learning how to turn information into useful insights.
    • Building projects and developing my technical skills, one step at a time.
    • Rethinking what I want from my education and career.
    • Working towards more independence and a future that feels right for me.
    • Collecting little memories, finding new music, and figuring things out as I go.

  2. Systems, and people

    I’m analytical. I like structure, evidence and working things out. But I also pay attention to tone, symbolism and the meaning behind little things. I care about how something is said, not just what is said, and I can usually tell when words don’t quite match what someone means.

    I’m interested in systems, and I’m interested in people. I want things to make sense logically, and I want them to feel right.

  3. Things I want to stay true to

    Faith
    An important part of who I am and how I approach life.
    Authenticity
    Being honest about who I am, what I know, and what I’m still learning.
    Justice
    Standing with Palestine. Some things matter more than being liked by everyone.
    Independence
    Building a life where I support myself, keep studying and make my own decisions.
    Growth
    Being willing to learn, change my mind, and start over when I need to.

  4. On starting over

    I’ve learned that changing direction doesn’t mean you’ve failed at the original plan. Sometimes you learn more about yourself by recognising that something no longer fits. I’m still figuring things out, but I’m learning to trust the process of getting there, even when it doesn’t look exactly how I imagined it.

  6. A few reminders

    • I’d rather understand something properly than pretend I know it.
    • I want my work to be useful, not just impressive.
    • I can be ambitious without having everything figured out.
    • Changing my mind when I learn something new is allowed.
    • I want to build a life that reflects my values, not just other people’s expectations.
    • Not everything that matters to me needs to be shared publicly.

  7. Things I’m figuring out

    • How to build a career that challenges me without letting it become my entire identity.
    • How to keep learning without feeling like I need to know everything already.
    • How to make decisions based on the life I want, rather than the path that looks best to everyone else.
    • How to appreciate people and experiences without feeling the need to hold on to everything forever.
    • How to trust that a life can still be good when it doesn’t follow the timeline I once imagined.
